Hospital Performance Dashboard

Designed and developed an interactive Power BI Hospital Dashboard analyzing 1,000 patient records to uncover trends in patient flow, readmissions, treatment costs, length of stay, and clinical outcomes. The dashboard provides actionable insights for healthcare decision-makers by identifying high-risk departments, vulnerable patient groups, seasonal readmission patterns, and cost-to-outcome inefficiencies. Recommendations were delivered to improve patient outcomes, reduce avoidable readmissions, and optimize resource utilization.

Key Metrics & KPIs

Total Patients: 1,000
Average Length of Stay (LOS): 7.46 days
Average Treatment Cost: ₦330.28K
Overall Readmission Rate: 21%
Department-wise Patient Volume Analysis
Readmission Rate by Department, Month, and Age Group
Treatment Cost vs. Patient Outcome Analysis
High-Cost, Poor-Outcome Department Identification
Disease Burden Distribution Across Diagnoses
Length of Stay Impact on Readmissions
